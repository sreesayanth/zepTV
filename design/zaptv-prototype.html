ZapTV

UI/UX prototype · Android TV · v0.1

Click the TV, then use your keyboard, or press the buttons on the remote. It behaves the way the app will: live TV first, a channel banner the instant you press a key, and every screen reachable with only the arrows, OK and BACK. The channels are a sample Malayalam lineup with made-up schedules, and the picture is animated artwork, not real streams.

Design spec

The rules behind the screens

Everything inside the TV is measured in Android TV dp: a 1080p screen is 960 × 540 dp. These values go straight into the Compose theme. The full written spec is UI_UX_DESIGN.md in the project folder.

Six rules

  • Video first.Live TV is the home screen. Every menu opens over the picture, which keeps playing.
  • The smallest remote reaches everything.The Google TV remote has only the D-pad, OK and BACK. Guide, search and settings all live in Options (▶). Extra keys are shortcuts.
  • Feedback within 100 ms.The channel banner appears on the key press, before the stream loads, so switching feels instant.
  • Never a black screen.Loading, failure and digital-only channels each get a clear message and a way out.
  • Readable from the sofa.Nothing smaller than 12 dp, main text 15–21 dp, a cream focus fill with a gold ring, and a Large text setting.
  • Two languages, one layout.Every label exists in English and Malayalam. Layouts leave room for Malayalam strings, which run about 30% longer.

Focus

Focus is the cursor on a TV, so it has to be obvious from three metres away: a cream fill, a 2 dp gold ring, a 3% lift and a shadow. The playing channel keeps a gold number and moving bars.

Layout and safe area

Many TVs crop the picture edges (overscan), so text and controls stay inside 48 dp at the sides and 27 dp at the top and bottom. The grid is 8 dp, list rows are 46 dp and corners are 10–16 dp.
